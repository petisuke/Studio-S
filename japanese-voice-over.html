Best For

When you need Japanese audio that fits the edit.

This service is suited for global production teams creating Japanese versions of brand films, product explainers, mobile game ads, YouTube videos, app tutorials, corporate content, and online campaigns.

Recording Setup

Remote Japanese voice recording from a dedicated booth in Japan.

Recording is handled with a Neumann U87Ai microphone, RME Fireface UCX audio interface, and a treated soundproof booth. Clean WAV, AIFF, MP3, or project-specific delivery formats can be prepared depending on your production workflow.

The voice direction can be adjusted for bright commercial reads, natural narration, calm corporate tone, mobile game ads, app explainers, or timing-aware voice over for edited video. English email communication is available for international production teams.
