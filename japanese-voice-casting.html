Casting Scope

Useful for dialogue, characters, and multi-role videos.

Casting support can be useful for male-female dialogue, app walkthroughs, explainers, anime-style videos, short drama, character voice, and projects with several roles or age ranges.

Partner and team reels are not posted publicly by default. Relevant voice options can be shared privately after an inquiry, depending on the project brief and availability.
